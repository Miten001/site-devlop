How FlexFam cloud mining works

Rent hashrate, earn continuously, claim whenever you like.

1 Get hashrate. Start the free starter rig, or buy a plan with USDT from your wallet or with points earned from tasks and campaigns.
2 The rig mines 24/7. Rewards accrue every second in real time and keep accumulating while you are offline or the tab is closed.
3 Invest boost. Any rig you buy for $10 or more mines +20% faster for its entire term — the boost is locked in the moment you purchase.
4 Boost it daily. One free +25% speed boost every 24 hours, active for 8 hours — it stacks on top of the invest boost.
5 Claim your rewards. Send the mined balance to your USDT wallet, or take it as points with a +10% bonus.
Mining is not a guaranteed income. Output depends on network difficulty and pool performance, contracts expire at the end of their term, and hashrate purchases are non-refundable. Never spend more than you can afford to lose.
