How FlexFam works

FlexFam connects members who complete small community tasks with creators who post campaigns. There are two different task flows: points campaigns and the USDT Task Market. Read the requirements and reward before starting.

Complete a community campaign

  1. Create an account and open Earn Points.
  2. Choose an available campaign. Its destination opens in a new tab, and a seven-second timer runs on FlexFam.
  3. Once the wait and server checks complete, eligible points are credited. Already completed campaigns disappear from your available list.

This timed visit does not verify that somebody followed, subscribed or made a purchase. Campaign availability, funding and completion limits still apply.

Work on a Task Market job

Task Market jobs have instructions, a reward and available worker slots. Follow the poster's requirements and submit your proof. If a screenshot is requested, attach a readable image without exposing passwords, verification codes or other sensitive information.

The poster reviews the proof before approving or rejecting it. Submission is not the same as payment approval.

Post your own campaign or task

Use Add Page for a community campaign or Post a Task for a job requiring proof review. Set a clear budget and requirements. Task Market jobs use funded escrow, and posters can review submissions from My Tasks.

Community visits are incentivized traffic. Do not advertise them as guaranteed sales, verified followers or organic engagement. Check the destination platform's rules before posting.

Understand points and withdrawals

Check the Wallet page for current conversion rules, minimum withdrawal amount, supported payment methods and fees before spending time or money. A displayed points balance does not promise an immediate cash payout. Requests may require admin review.

Rewards and availability vary. FlexFam tasks are not employment, and no daily income or investment return is guaranteed.

Common questions

Do all tasks need a screenshot?

No. Timed community campaigns do not need screenshots. Task Market jobs can require screenshots or other proof specified by the poster.

Can task posters see submitted screenshots?

Yes. Posters can open View proof in My Tasks and review the submitted text, link and attached screenshot.

Where can I get help?

Sign in and open Support Tickets to explain your account, task, deposit or withdrawal issue. Include a reference ID, not your password.
